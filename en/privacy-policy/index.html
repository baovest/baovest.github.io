Effective date: 24 November 2026

Baovest Labs ("we", “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and related services (such as ebooks, webinars, newsletters, and landing pages), why we collect it, and the choices you have.

We are based in Singapore and handle personal data in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). Where other laws apply to you, such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we also respect the rights those laws give you.

1. Data We Collect

Information you give us

  • Name and email address, when you sign up for an ebook, webinar, newsletter, or other resource.
  • Information you choose to include when you contact us or answer optional questions on a sign-up form (for example, your trading experience or interests).
  • Webinar participation details, such as attendance and questions you ask during a session.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the referring page. Our hosting provider and content delivery services record this in standard server logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We currently use only what the site needs to work and to remember your preferences (for example, light or dark mode, stored in your browser). If we add analytics or advertising in the future (for example, Google Analytics or Google AdSense), those services may set cookies, and we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive data (such as NRIC numbers, financial account details, or health information). Please do not send us such data.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver what you requested, such as an ebook download link or webinar access.
  • To send you newsletters, market insights, and information about our resources, events, and offerings, if you agreed to receive them.
  • To reply to your questions and requests.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve our content.
  • To keep the site secure and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Consent and Legal Basis

Under the PDPA, we collect, use, and disclose your personal data with your consent, or where the law otherwise allows. When you submit a form, you agree to the purposes stated on that form and in this policy.

If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on: your consent (for marketing emails), performance of a request you made (for example, sending an ebook you asked for), and our legitimate interests (for site security and improvement).

4. Marketing Emails

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (see section 11). We will stop sending marketing emails within the time required by law, and in any case within 10 business days.

We do not send marketing messages by phone call, SMS, or messaging apps. If we ever do, we will comply with Singapore’s Do Not Call (DNC) rules.

5. Sharing Your Data

We share personal data only with trusted service providers who help us run our services, such as:

  • Website hosting and content delivery (for example, GitHub Pages and content delivery networks).
  • Email and newsletter platforms, to store your subscription and send emails.
  • Webinar platforms, to register you and run the session.
  • Analytics or advertising providers, if enabled in the future (for example, Google).

These providers may use your data only to provide services to us. We may also disclose data if required by law, or in connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or sale), in which case this policy will continue to protect your data.

6. International Transfers

Some of our service providers store or process data outside Singapore (for example, in the United States). When we transfer data overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives protection comparable to that under the PDPA, for example by using providers bound by contractual data protection terms.

7. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above:

  • Mailing list data: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We may keep a minimal record (your email address) to make sure we do not contact you again.
  • Webinar and enquiry data: typically no longer than 2 years after your last interaction, unless the law requires otherwise.
  • Server logs: as set by our hosting providers, usually a short period.

8. How We Protect Your Data

We use reasonable security measures to protect your personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), reputable service providers, and limited access to the data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a data breach where the law requires it.

9. Your Rights

Under the PDPA, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and ask how it has been used or disclosed in the past year.
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for us to collect, use, or disclose your data. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide certain services (such as sending you resources).

If you are in the EU, UK, or another region with similar laws, you may also ask us to delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, or receive a portable copy of it. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. In Singapore, this is the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 11. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time required by law (generally within 30 days).

10. Children

Our website and services are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the effective date at the top. If we make significant changes, we will let you know by email or a notice on the website.
